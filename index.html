// RACE REGULATIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who is eligible to participate in SYNORA 2026?

SYNORA is open to all university freshers (1st year undergraduate students) across all branches and degree programs. Beginners with zero prior hackathon experience are warmly encouraged to join!

What is the team size limit?

Teams can have 2 to 4 members. You can register as an already formed team or register individually and match with other racers in our Discord Pitlane.

Is there any registration fee?

No! Registration is 100% free. Food, refreshments, high-speed Wi-Fi, mentorship, and swags are fully provided for all accepted participants.

Can I use existing projects or boilerplate code?

All project code must be authored during the 36-hour sprint window. You are welcome to utilize open-source libraries, frameworks, APIs, and AI coding assistants.
